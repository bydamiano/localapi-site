Download for macOS (.dmg)
macOS 13 Ventura or laterApple Silicon & IntelFree

Install in three steps

  1. Open the downloaded LocalAPI-x.y.z.dmg.
  2. Drag LocalAPI onto the Applications folder in the window that opens.
  3. Open LocalAPI from Applications or Launchpad.

First launch only: LocalAPI is not signed with a paid Apple certificate, so macOS may say it can't verify the developer. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
